# Travel and Expense Policy

> Fictional expense policy of Lumenfold Labs for the DocMind demo.

## General rules

Spend company money as if it were your own. Expenses are submitted in the expense tool with a photo of the receipt within 30 days of the purchase. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly salary.

Expenses above 250 EUR need approval from your manager before the purchase.

## Travel

Book flights and hotels through the travel desk at least 14 days in advance when possible. Economy class is the default for flights shorter than six hours; for longer flights, premium economy is allowed.

Hotels should cost no more than 170 EUR per night in most cities, or 230 EUR per night in expensive cities such as London, Zurich or New York.

## Meals and daily allowance

When travelling for work, you receive a daily meal allowance of 45 EUR. Team dinners during company events are paid by the company and do not reduce the allowance. Alcohol is not reimbursed except at official company events.

## Home office and learning

Home office purchases and learning expenses follow the budgets in the Employee Handbook. Submit them in the expense tool under the matching category.

## Non-reimbursable expenses

Fines, personal entertainment subscriptions, upgrades paid for personal comfort and lost-luggage fees without an airline report are not reimbursed.
