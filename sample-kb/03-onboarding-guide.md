# Onboarding Guide

> Fictional onboarding guide of Lumenfold Labs for the DocMind demo.

## Before day one

People Operations sends your laptop so that it arrives at least two working days before your start date. You will receive an invitation to set up SSO and your password manager on your personal email.

## First week

- Day 1: welcome call with your manager, SSO and security-key setup, introduction to your onboarding buddy.
- Day 2: product overview and architecture walkthrough.
- Day 3: set up the development environment using the "Getting started" repository.
- Day 4: pair-programming session with your buddy on a small real task.
- Day 5: first retrospective with your manager: what went well, what was confusing.

## Onboarding buddy

Every new hire gets an onboarding buddy from a neighbouring team for the first 60 days. The buddy answers "how do we do things here" questions and meets you at least twice a week during the first month.

## First 90 days

Your manager agrees on 30/60/90-day goals with you in the first two weeks. At the end of the 90 days there is a structured feedback conversation in both directions.

## Tools

We use a chat workspace for day-to-day communication, a shared wiki for documentation, an issue tracker for planning and a code hosting platform for all repositories. Access to all of them is granted automatically through SSO on day one.
